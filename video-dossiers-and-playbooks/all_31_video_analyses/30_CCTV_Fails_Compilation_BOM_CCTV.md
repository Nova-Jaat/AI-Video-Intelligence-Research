## 3. The Hard Truth: Why This Will NOT Make You Money

**YouTube's "Reused Content" Policy:**
- YouTube explicitly states that channels that "simply re-upload clips from other creators without sufficient added value" are ineligible for monetization, even if technically uploaded as original files.
- Signs your compilation channel will be demonetized:
  - No original narration explaining the clips.
  - No consistent educational or transformative perspective.
  - Clips sourced from Reddit, Twitter, Douyin (Chinese TikTok), or other social media without proper licensing.

**Copyright Strikes:**
- Many CCTV clips originate from CCTV system owners or broadcasting companies who have registered their footage with YouTube's Content ID system.
- 3 copyright strikes within 90 days = **permanent account deletion**.

**Realistic Revenue:**
- Low RPM: Entertainment/fail compilation category: **$0.50 – $1.50 per 1,000 views**.
- 1 million views = **$500 to $1,500**. But reaching 1M views requires viral distribution, which is unreliable and algorithmically unpredictable.
