## 2. Executive Summary
Davie Fogarty (founder of The Oodie, an eight-figure wearable blanket brand) shares his high-level promotional and operational playbook for Black Friday / Cyber Monday (BFCM). The masterclass covers multi-million dollar inventory forecasting, building a 100k+ subscriber VIP early access email list, aggressive tiered bundle discounts, and maximizing customer lifetime value (LTV).

---

## 3. The Money Math & Economic Reality
- **Q4 Retail Physics:** E-commerce brands generate 40% to 65% of their total annual net profit in the 6-week window between November 1 and mid-December.
- **Tiered Bundle Architecture:**
  - Flat discounts (e.g. 20% off site-wide) destroy gross margins.
  - Tiered discounts drive huge Average Order Value (AOV):
    - Buy 1: 15% off (AOV: $85)
    - Buy 2: 25% off (AOV: $150)
    - Buy 4: 40% off (AOV: $240)
- **Capital Reality:** Running this playbook requires purchasing $50,000 to $500,000 in physical inventory 4 months in advance from overseas manufacturers, plus maintaining a $5,000/day ad spend budget.

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Implementation Blueprint
1. **Phase 1: VIP List Building (October):**
   - Run lead generation ads on Meta offering exclusive 2-hour early access and the biggest discount of the year to VIP email and SMS subscribers.
2. **Phase 2: The VIP Drop (Early November):**
   - Send password-protected early access to VIPs 48 hours before the public sale.
3. **Phase 3: The Public Blitz:**
   - Launch dynamic countdown timers on Shopify.
   - Set up automated SMS abandonment reminders sent within 15 minutes of cart abandonment.
4. **Phase 4: Post-Cyber Monday Clean-Up:**
   - Retarget shoppers who viewed items but didn't buy with "Last Chance Mystery Stock Clearance".

### Critical Pitfalls & Hidden Traps:
- Cash-flow lockup: Buying physical inventory that fails to sell, leaving you with thousands of dollars of unsellable stock in your garage.
- Ad platform CPC spikes: Advertising costs on Facebook and TikTok double or triple during Black Friday due to corporate competition (Nike, Walmart).
