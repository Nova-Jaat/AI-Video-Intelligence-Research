## 2. Executive Summary
Ty Chen reveals how he scaled a specialized AI automation tool from zero to $10,000 in monthly recurring revenue (MRR) within 30 days by completely ignoring paid advertising, viral social media, and product-led growth. His entire distribution strategy was **hyper-personalized cold email and LinkedIn voice note outreach** targeting a hyper-specific buyer persona.

---

## 3. The Money Math & Economic Reality
**The B2B Outbound Math:**
- Ty sends 60 highly personalized messages per day.
- 5% reply rate = 3 replies per day.
- 50% of replies schedule a demo = 1.5 demos per day.
- 25% of demos convert to paying customers.
- Pricing: $200 to $500/month per customer.
- After 30 days: 10+ new paying customers = **$2,000 to $5,000 MRR minimum**.
- With 3-4 sales per week at $500/month each, Month 2 compounds rapidly toward **$10,000 MRR**.

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Implementation Blueprint
1. **Step 1: Identify Exactly ONE Target Buyer:**
   - Don't try to sell to "businesses". Narrow it down: *"Marketing Agency Owners with 2 to 15 employees who currently use Zapier for client reporting workflows."*
2. **Step 2: Build Your ICP Lead List (200 to 500 names):**
   - Use Apollo.io free tier (50 exports/month) to filter by company size, industry, and job title.
3. **Step 3: Hyper-Personalized Video Outreach (Loom):**
   - Record a 60-second screen recording showing THEIR specific website, with the opening line: *"I found your agency on LinkedIn while researching who is managing Shopify client reporting. I noticed you are still using manual dashboards, which is costing your team 4 hours per week. I built a demo showing what your agency's automated weekly report could look like in 30 seconds: [show demo]."*
4. **Step 4: Irresistible Reverse Risk Offer:**
   - *"Try it completely free for 2 weeks. If your team doesn't save at least 3 hours per week, cancel with 1 click and you owe me nothing."*
