## 4. Step-by-Step Implementation Blueprint
1. **Step 1: Set Up a Vapi.ai Account:**
   - Create a free Vapi account and deploy their starter phone agent template.
2. **Step 2: Connect a Twilio Phone Number:**
   - Purchase a local phone number ($1.15/month) in the client's area code.
3. **Step 3: Configure the Agent Script:**
   - Write custom dialogue flows: *"Hello! You've reached [Business Name]. I'm an AI assistant helping with scheduling. Are you calling to book an appointment or ask about services?"*
4. **Step 4: Connect to Calendar:**
   - Integrate Vapi agent actions with Cal.com or Google Calendar API for automatic appointment booking.
5. **Step 5: Pitch with a Live Demo:**
   - Call the demo number yourself and show the business owner a recording of the AI handling a realistic patient inquiry.
