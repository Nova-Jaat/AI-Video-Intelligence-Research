## 4. Step-by-Step Implementation Blueprint

### The 3 Mandatory Automated Flows:
1. **Flow 1: The Welcome Sequence (5-Email Series over 7 Days)**
   - Email 1 (Instant): Welcome, fulfill promised offer or discount, establish brand voice.
   - Email 2 (Day 2): Founder origin story / "why we do what we do".
   - Email 3 (Day 3): Social proof – testimonials, before/after, media features.
   - Email 4 (Day 5): Best-seller deep-dive with direct purchase link.
   - Email 5 (Day 7): Urgency or scarcity nudge ("Last 48 hours for new subscriber discount").
   
2. **Flow 2: The Abandoned Cart Recovery (3-Email Series)**
   - Email 1 (1 hour): *"Hey, you forgot something"* – Light and casual reminder.
   - Email 2 (24 hours): Address common objections (risk-free returns, secure checkout).
   - Email 3 (48 hours): Strong urgency + small discount to tip the buyer.

3. **Flow 3: The 60-Day Winback / Re-Engagement**
   - Target subscribers who haven't opened or clicked in 60 days.
   - Subject line: *"Are we breaking up?"* or *"We miss you – here's a secret"*.
   - Suppress cold subscribers who don't re-engage (keep list healthy and deliverability high).
