## 4. Step-by-Step 30-Day Blueprint

### Days 1-7: Specialization & Tool Mastery
- Choose ONE high-ticket service industry: Real Estate (lead qualification), Solar (speed-to-lead), Med Spas (appointment recovery), Law Firms (intake automation), or Mortgage Brokers (pre-qualification).
- Build a demo AI automation using Claude API + Make.com that solves the industry's #1 pain point (slow lead response time).

### Days 8-14: Outbound Campaign Launch
- Build a list of 300 target businesses using Apollo.io filtered by industry, employee count (10-50), and estimated annual revenue.
- Send 50 highly personalized outbound emails per day using Instantly.ai with a proven cold email framework:

**Subject:** "[First Name] - saw you're doing [specific thing they're doing]"

**Body:** *"Hey [First Name], I noticed your team [specific business detail]. I work with [industry] businesses to automate their lead qualification process using AI - typically reducing follow-up time from 24+ hours to under 60 seconds. I built a quick demo showing what this could look like for [Company Name]. Would it be okay if I sent it over? Takes 3 minutes to watch."*

### Days 15-25: Close & Deliver
- For every demo request, send a personalized Loom video showing the AI bot handling their specific business scenario.
- Present a simple 2-tier offer: $2,500 Setup + $1,000/month OR $4,000 Setup + $750/month.

### Days 26-30: Optimize & Scale
- Hire a freelance VA to handle all prospecting list building.
- Focus your time only on demos, closing, and client onboarding.
