## 4. Step-by-Step Implementation Blueprint
1. **Create a Visually Consistent AI Persona:**
   - Use Midjourney or Fooocus to generate 50 images of a single consistent character with the same face, hair style, and body proportions.
   - Use one of these image settings tools to "fix" the face seed so all future generations maintain the same identity.
2. **Hardware and Technical Setup for US Distribution:**
   - New phone or factory-reset existing phone.
   - US eSIM (from companies like Airalo or USMobile, costing $5–$15 for 30 days of US data).
   - Set device timezone to US Eastern or Pacific time.
   - Set device language to US English.
   - Create the Instagram/Facebook account while connected exclusively via the US eSIM data.
3. **Content Signals that Tell Instagram to Serve US Audiences:**
   - Use exclusively trending US audio (from US creators' viral reels).
   - Tag US locations (Los Angeles, Miami Beach, New York City) in every post.
   - Use US-centric hashtags and time publishing to 7 PM – 10 PM US Eastern.
4. **Monetization Paths:**
   - US brand deals (via CreatorIQ, AspireIQ).
   - Fanvue or Patreon memberships ($10 – $29/month per subscriber).
   - ClickBank or high-converting US-only affiliate offers.
