## 2. Executive Summary
Patrick details how creators generate $5,000 to $20,000 per day by posting AI-generated product review and demonstration videos with TikTok's integrated "Shop" checkout yellow basket link, earning 10% to 30% commissions on impulse-driven physical product sales directly within the TikTok app. This model eliminates shipping, inventory, and customer service responsibilities entirely.

## 3. The Money Math & Economic Reality
**TikTok Shop Affiliate Commission Structure:**
- Average viral physical product: $25 to $45 retail price.
- Commission rate: 10% to 20% = **$2.50 to $9 per unit sold**.
- A video receiving 2 million organic views during peak trend:
  - Purchase conversion: 0.08% = **1,600 units sold**.
  - At $6 average commission = **$9,600 from a single video**.
- Replicable: Posting 5 videos daily with 1 going semi-viral (200k-500k views) = **$500 to $2,500/day consistently**.

## 4. Step-by-Step Implementation Blueprint

### Part 1: TikTok Shop Affiliate Setup
1. Apply for TikTok Shop Creator Affiliate Program.
   - Requirement: 5,000 followers OR be a registered US TikTok Shop seller.
   - For non-US creators: Create a US-based LLC or partner with a US resident.
2. Navigate to TikTok Creator Center -> Affiliate Products -> Shop.
3. Filter by: High Commission (>15%), High GMV (revenue volume), Rising Trend.

### Part 2: AI Video Creation Workflow
1. **Product Research:** Use FastMoss.com or Kalodata.com to identify products with accelerating daily order volume.
2. **Script Generation:** Ask Claude: *"Write 5 TikTok-style 20-second product demonstration scripts for [Product Name]. Each script opens with a problem-agitation hook and closes with a curiosity-based soft CTA: 'You can grab this directly in the shop link below.'"*
3. **AI Avatar Creation (Optional):** Use Creatify.ai or HeyGen to generate a photorealistic AI avatar speaking the script.
4. **Authenticity Check:** Add real-looking B-roll: package unboxing, hands-on product interaction (from manufacturer media kit or creative commons sources).
5. **Post with Product Tag:** Add the yellow TikTok Shop basket tag to the video and in the caption.
