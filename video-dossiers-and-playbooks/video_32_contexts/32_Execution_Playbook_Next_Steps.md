## 🎯 14-Day Sprint Goal
Select the single highest-probability model for your current assets, deploy a zero-friction outcome-based offer, secure your first paying client or 50 founding community members, and generate **$1,000 – $3,000 in revenue**.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Decision Matrix — Which Context Should YOU Launch Today?

Answer these 3 simple questions:

1. **How much upfront capital do you have?**
   - **$0 to $100:** Choose **[Context 08 (Outcomes)](./08_Context_Outcomes_Automated_AI_Service_Agencies.md)** or **[Context 03 (Attention)](./03_Context_Attention_Micro_Niche_Media_Engine.md)**.
   - **$500 to $2,000:** Choose **[Context 05 (Belonging)](./05_Context_Belonging_Paid_Communities_IRL_Events.md)** or **[Context 07 (Unique Data)](./07_Context_Unique_Data_Proprietary_Databases.md)**.
   - **$5,000+:** Look into **[Context 01 (Atoms JV)](./01_Context_Atoms_Physical_Businesses.md)** or **[Context 06 (Marketplaces)](./06_Context_Liquidity_AI_Driven_Niche_Marketplaces.md)**.

2. **What is your technical skill level?**
   - **Non-Technical / Pure Communicator:** Go with **Context 05 (Belonging)** or **Context 03 (Attention)**.
   - **Prompt Engineer / Low-Code / Builder:** Go with **Context 08 (Outcomes)** or **Context 04 (Timing)**.
   - **Coder / Data Scraper:** Go with **Context 07 (Unique Data)** or **Context 06 (Liquidity)**.

3. **When do you need your first dollar?**
   - **Within 7 Days:** **Context 08 (Outcomes)** — zero hesitation.
   - **Within 30 Days:** **Context 05 (Belonging)** or **Context 04 (Timing)**.
   - **Long-Term Enterprise Moat (6+ months):** **Context 02 (Permission)** or **Context 01 (Atoms)**.

## 📝 Step 2: Copy-Paste Outreach Scripts

```text
Subject: quick question regarding [Company Name] operations

Hey [First Name],

I was reviewing your job postings and noticed you're looking to hire for [Specific Painful Task: e.g. lead prospecting / manual invoice auditing / data migration].

Instead of spending $3,500/month on human payroll, we built an automated AI workflow that delivers [Guaranteed Tangible Result: e.g. 50 verified qualified B2B leads / 100% processed invoices] directly to your inbox every Monday morning.

We'll run your first batch completely free this week so you can test the quality. If you like the results, it's just a flat $500/month to keep it running. If not, you pay nothing.

Mind if I send over a quick 60-second video showing how it works?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## 📅 Step 3: The 14-Day Day-by-Day Launch Schedule

### Week 1: Setup & Prospecting
- **Day 1:** Pick your primary Context (Recommended: **Context 08** for fast cash or **Context 05** for recurring revenue).
- **Day 2:** Build your master fulfillment workflow (n8n, Claude Code script, or Skool setup).
- **Day 3:** Create your Stripe payment checkout link ($350 for initial outcome project or $99/mo subscription).
- **Day 4:** Build a targeted list of 50 qualified prospects using Apollo.io or LinkedIn.
- **Day 5:** Send 25 personalized Spear Emails / DMs.
- **Day 6-7:** Handle inbound replies and send 60-second Loom demonstration previews.

### Week 2: Closing & Fulfillment
- **Day 8:** Close Client #1 with 100% risk reversal: *"Pay only upon delivery."*
- **Day 9-10:** Run your autonomous AI agent pipeline to fulfill the deliverable in record time.
- **Day 11:** Deliver the finished result with an itemized report and your Stripe invoice link.
- **Day 12:** Collect the first payment ($350 – $1,000) and request a 2-sentence written testimonial.
- **Day 13:** Send the remaining 25 outbound pitches, featuring your new testimonial as social proof.
- **Day 14:** Pitch the first client on an ongoing monthly retainer ($500/month) to automate the service permanently.
