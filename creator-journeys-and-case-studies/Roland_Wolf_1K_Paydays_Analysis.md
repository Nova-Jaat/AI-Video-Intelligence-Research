1. **Gap & Crap Strategy:**
   - Roland's primary setup targeting morning gap-ups in low-float small-caps and penny stocks.
   - Identifies exhaustion points and signs of volume fading at market open to take short or fade positions.
2. **Tape Reading & Level 2:**
   - Interpreting order book dynamics, bidder support, spoof orders, and wall absorption during high-volume spikes.
3. **Execution & Risk Management:**
   - Strict stop-loss rules designed to prevent catastrophic drawdowns on volatile low-float runners.
   - Sizing positions around profit goals (targeting regular daily milestones like the marketed "$1K payday").
