## 4. Step-by-Step Implementation Blueprint
1. **Stage 1: AI Rough Cut (60% of editing time saved):**
   - Import raw footage into **Gling.ai** (auto-removes all silences, long pauses, and filler words "um", "uh", "like").
   - Export the auto-cut timeline directly into DaVinci Resolve or Premiere Pro.
2. **Stage 2: Human Creative Layer (40% of editing time, 100% of quality):**
   - Add human reaction zoom cuts at key punchlines.
   - Choose contextually appropriate background music and adjust emotional pacing.
   - Insert b-roll clips, screen recordings, or animated text graphics that reinforce the script's key argument.
3. **Stage 3: Automated Caption Generation:**
   - Use CapCut desktop Auto Captions: exports SRT or burned-in animated captions in under 2 minutes.
4. **Stage 4: Thumbnail & Export:**
   - Canva for thumbnail with high-contrast text.
   - Export in 4K at 50 Mbps for YouTube's highest encoding quality.
