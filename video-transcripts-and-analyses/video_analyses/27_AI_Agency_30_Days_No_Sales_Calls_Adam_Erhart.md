## 2. Executive Summary
Adam Erhart presents the most introvert-friendly, rejection-resistant client acquisition framework for starting an AI agency: the **Proof-First, Async Video Close method**. By eliminating high-pressure cold calls and replacing them with a 2-step asynchronous process (Spear Email + Loom Audit Video), even complete beginners can land $1,000 to $3,000 agency retainers without ever being on a live sales call.

## 3. The Money Math & Economic Reality
**Why Async Closing Works:**
- Traditional sales: 90-minute discovery call → 3-day proposal writing → 15-minute close call → 50% of clients ghost. Total effort: 10 hours per conversion.
- Async Closing: 60-second Spear Email → 3-minute Loom audit → 1-click Stripe payment link. Total effort: **1 hour per client**.
- Closing 2 clients per week at $1,500 each = **$3,000/week = $12,000/month**.

## 4. The Exact Framework

### Step 1: The "Permission-First" Spear Email
*"Hey [First Name],*

*I spotted your website while looking for [business type] in [city]. Noticed your [specific specific flaw - no mobile CTA, slow load time, no appointment button, etc.].*

*I made a quick 90-second video walking through the exact fix. It's ready if you want me to send it over—totally free. No strings attached.*

*Worth a look?"*

### Step 2: When They Say "Sure" or "Yes"...
Send the Loom Video (3 minutes max):
1. *Open their actual website and show the specific problem they don't know exists.*
2. *Show a 30-second preview of the fix or solution (a mockup, a code snippet result, or the AI bot demo).*
3. *Close: "If you'd like my team to implement this for you, here is the checkout link. We'll have it done by [specific Friday/Tuesday date]. No contracts, cancel anytime."*

### Step 3: The 1-Click Stripe Payment
- Create a Stripe Payment Link for each of your service tiers.
- After the Loom, include the direct payment URL in your email:
  *"Here's the link to get started: [stripe.com/pay/your-link]"*

### Step 4: What to Offer
- **Tier 1 ($500 flat):** Website Speed & Mobile Fix + 1 SEO improvement.
- **Tier 2 ($1,500 flat):** Full website rebuild on a modern template.
- **Tier 3 ($500/month recurring):** Monthly social media content creation using AI.

## 7. Action Plan for a Complete Beginner
```markdown
Day 1: Create a free Loom account. Create a Stripe account and your first payment link ($500).
Day 2: Identify 25 local service businesses with weak websites (roofers, plumbers, dentists, cleaning services).

Day 4: When you get 2-3 "yes" replies, record personalized Looms for each.
Day 5: Send the Stripe payment link with the Loom. Close your first client.
```
