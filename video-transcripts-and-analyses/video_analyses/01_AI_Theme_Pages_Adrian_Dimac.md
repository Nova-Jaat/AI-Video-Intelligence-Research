## 4. The 5-Step Masterclass Implementation Blueprint

Rather than low-margin dropshipping, digital print-on-demand, or building complex SaaS from scratch, leverage established enterprise health and beauty brands via **Levanta.io**.

* **Selection Criteria:**
  * **Net Profit:** $20 – $40 profit per unit sold.
  * **Commission Rate:** Minimum 20%–35%+ (higher tier agreements can reach up to 50%).
  * **Niche Urgency:** High emotional pain points where consumers actively seek immediate solutions (gut health, severe bloating, constipation, adult acne, hormonal skin, anti-aging).
* **Live Case Examples Analyzed in Video:**
  1. *Physician's Choice 60 Billion Probiotic:* Retails at $49.98. Earns $14.99 to $20+ commission per conversion.
  2. *Solawave Red Light Eye Therapy Mask:* High-ticket beauty device earning ~$49.80 profit per unit.
* **Execution:** Create a publisher account on Levanta, apply to brand programs, and obtain your customized affiliate tracking links.

### Step 2: Competitor & Market Research (Pre-Content Phase)
**Golden Rule:** Never invent content concepts from scratch. Identify proven viral outliers and reverse-engineer their structure.

* **Research Stack & Workflow:**
  * **ViralHunter.app:** Filter by specific niche buckets (e.g., *Health*, *Beauty*) sorted by total view count. Look for proven viral formats:
    * *Transformation Timelines:* "Day 1 vs. Day 14 vs. Day 30 vs. Day 90".
    * *Authority Ratings:* "Rating Costco / Sephora Gut Health Items as a Functional Nutritionist".
    * *Product Reveal Transitions:* Reviewing common retail items before revealing the hero solution.
  * **Competitor Benchmarking:** Find 5 active competitor pages (e.g., *Siana Health*) promoting the same or similar affiliate supplements.
  * **ChatGPT Pain-Point Mapping:** Query ChatGPT on underlying biological symptoms (e.g., gut-skin axis, eczema flare-ups, chronic fatigue) to generate fresh visual angles and storytelling hooks.

### Step 3: Account Setup, Branding & The 3-Day Warm-Up Protocol
Adrian builds a brand from scratch named **"Wang Remedy"** to demonstrate the setup:

1. **Omnichannel Setup:**
   * Create dedicated accounts on **Instagram** (switched to Creator/Professional account), **TikTok**, and a **Facebook Page**.
2. **Bio Copywriting:**
   * Prompt ChatGPT: *"Rewrite this high-converting competitor Instagram bio for an authoritative male functional nutritionist persona."*
   * Include clear authority tags, core benefits, and a call-to-action leading to the bio link or DM trigger.
3. **AI Persona Creation:**
   * Prompt ChatGPT / DALL-E: *"Create an AI avatar of an Asian male functional nutritionist, professional medical wellness setting, clear-frame glasses, subtle stubble beard, and refined styling."*
   * Keep this avatar identical across profile pictures on all three platforms to establish immediate authority.
4. **The Mandatory 3-Day Warm-Up Protocol (Anti-Shadowban):**
   * *Do NOT post any videos during the first 72 hours.* Algorithms flag instant posting on fresh accounts as automated spam.
   * Spend **10 minutes per day for 3 consecutive days** using a mobile device:
     * Search niche terms (`#guthealth`, `#probiotics`, `#bloating`).
     * Watch full reels and videos in your niche to train the algorithm.
     * Leave genuine, thoughtful comments and like 5–10 relevant posts daily.

### Step 4: AI Video Creation Workflow (Scene-by-Scene Recreation)
The video breaks down how to take a viral competitor video and systematically recreate it into a unique, high-retention video:

```
[Competitor Video] 
   └──> Download MP4 
   └──> Descript (Extract exact spoken script)
   └──> ChatGPT (Split script into 3-4 scenes + generate image prompts)
   └──> DALL-E / ChatGPT (Generate stills of AI Avatar holding products)
   └──> OpenArt.ai (Google Veo 3.1 Model: Animate stills into 9:16 UGC video)
   └──> CapCut (Stitch clips, adjust speed, add ratings & bold captions)
   └──> In-Video CTA ("Comment ALIVE for my morning gut stack")
```

1. **Script Extraction:** Download the competitor video and import it into **Descript** to extract the exact verbal transcript.
2. **Scene Prompts via ChatGPT:** Break down the script into visual beats.
   * *Example Opening Scene:* Avatar standing in Costco aisle stating: *"Rating Costco gut health items as a functional nutritionist. Half of what's on the shelves is marketing, only a fraction is worth your money..."*
3. **Image-to-Video Animation:**
   * Platform: **OpenArt.ai** using the **Google Veo 3.1** engine.
   * Settings: 9:16 vertical ratio, 1080p resolution, UGC talking-head motion prompt.
4. **CapCut Assembly:**
   * Stitch scenes together on the timeline.
   * Fine-tune playback speed (e.g., slow down or speed up specific clips to match natural conversational cadence).
   * Add prominent on-screen rating badges (e.g., *"Kirkland Chia Seeds: 9/10"*, *"Frozen Wild Blueberries: 8/10"*).
   * Generate bold, colorful auto-captions.
5. **The Comment CTA Hook:** Conclude every video with an explicit trigger keyword:
   > *"Comment **ALIVE** and I'll send you the exact morning protocol I've used for years."*

### Step 5: Automated 24/7 AI Sales Engine (EverSell.ai)
Replying manually to comments and direct messages is an administrative bottleneck. Adrian automates the entire conversion process using **EverSell.ai**:

1. **Account Integration:** Connect your Instagram Professional account and Facebook Page to EverSell.ai.
2. **Keyword Automation Trigger:** Set the "Magic Word" trigger to match your video CTA (e.g., `ALIVE`).
3. **Offer & Knowledge Base Setup:**
   * Input the product name (*Physician's Choice 60B Probiotics*), price ($49.98), and your Levanta affiliate tracking link.
   * Paste the full Amazon product description, key benefits, clinical strains, and FAQs directly into EverSell's AI training tab.
4. **Autonomous Consultative DM Selling:**
   * When a viewer comments `ALIVE`, EverSell automatically triggers an inbound direct message.
   * The AI acts as a caring wellness consultant: asks what symptoms they suffer from (bloating, fatigue, acne), explains how specific probiotic strains address their issues, and delivers the link naturally.
5. **Human-Paced Delay Setting:** Configure response latency to **"Human Mode" (~2-minute delay)**. Immediate bot-like replies reduce conversion rates; realistic typing delays build trust and rapport.

| **Upfront Capital Required** | **$0 – $50** | Free trials available for tools; main cost is AI generation credits & EverSell plan. |

### Critical Traps & Pitfalls:
1. **Skipping the 3-Day Warm-Up:** Posting immediately on a day-old Instagram or TikTok account triggers algorithmic spam suppression (stuck at 0–200 views).
2. **Promoting Low-Ticket Generic Products:** Attempting this model with a $2 Amazon commission requires impossible view counts. You must target items paying **$15 to $40+ per sale**.
3. **Falling into the Manual DM Trap:** Manually answering DMs caps your revenue and distracts from content production. Automated AI agents are mandatory for scale.
4. **Expecting Instant Riches on Day 3:** Content algorithms compound over 30 to 60 days. You are building an algorithmic asset, not buying a lottery ticket.
