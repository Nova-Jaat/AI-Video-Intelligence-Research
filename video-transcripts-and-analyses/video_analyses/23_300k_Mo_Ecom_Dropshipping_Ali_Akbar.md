## 3. The Money Math & Economic Reality
**Actual P&L of a $300k/Month Store:**
- Monthly Revenue: $300,000
- Product Cost (30% COGS): -$90,000
- Paid Ad Spend (25-35% of revenue): -$90,000
- Platform fees, fulfillment overhead, refunds: -$30,000
- **Net Profit (after all costs): $45,000 to $60,000/month (~15-20% margin)**
- To reach $300k revenue, the store spends $90,000/month on ads alone.

**Beginner Reality:**
- Starting a dropshipping store from scratch requires $1,500-$3,000 minimum in ad testing capital.
- 90% of beginner stores lose their entire test budget without finding a winning product.

## 4. Step-by-Step Implementation Blueprint
1. **Winning Product Research:**
   - Use Minea, Peeksta, or TikTok shop to find products with 100k+ TikTok sales and <3,000 reviews (not yet saturated).
2. **Private Agent Sourcing:**
   - Contact Yoybuy, CJ Dropshipping, or private Alibaba suppliers for branded packaging and 5-7 day air shipping.
3. **High-Converting One-Product Store:**
   - Shopify premium theme (Debutify/Booster), video hero banner, stacked testimonials widget, sticky add-to-cart.
4. **UGC Ad Testing:**
   - Request 3 UGC creator review videos from Billo.app or minisocial.com.
   - Test at $20/day on Meta for 3 days, kill losers, scale winners.

### Critical Pitfalls & Hidden Traps:
- **Capital destruction:** A beginner typically burns through $500 to $1,500 in failed ad tests before finding a winning product, if ever.
- **Account holds:** PayPal and Stripe routinely freeze payments from new stores for 90 days.
- **Chargeback risk:** Shipping delays cause chargebacks, which can permanently ban your Shopify store.

