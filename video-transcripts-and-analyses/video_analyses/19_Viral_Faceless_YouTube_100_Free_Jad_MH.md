## 4. Step-by-Step Implementation Blueprint
1. **Step 1: High-RPM Niche Selection:**
   - Avoid low-RPM niches: gaming ($1-$2 RPM), cooking ($2-$4 RPM), entertainment.
   - Target: Personal Finance ($10-$20 RPM), Investing ($12-$22 RPM), Marketing/Business ($8-$15 RPM).
2. **Step 2: Competitive Research:**
   - Use VidIQ or TubeBuddy free tier to find videos with high views but low subscriber channels (called "outlier" videos - proof the algorithm actively distributes this content).
3. **Step 3: AI-Powered Script Writing:**
   - Prompt Claude: *"Write a full 10-minute YouTube video script on 'How to Pay Off $30,000 in Debt Using the Debt Avalanche Method'. Include an open-loop hook in the first 30 seconds, 5 actionable steps with real examples, and a retention-driving cliffhanger at the 50% mark."*
4. **Step 4: Voiceover Recording:**
   - Use free ElevenLabs (10,000 characters/month) or CapCut's AI voice on desktop for a natural narration.
5. **Step 5: Visual Assembly:**
   - CapCut desktop timeline: Import the voiceover, overlay relevant stock footage from Pexels (financial charts, people working, credit cards), add dynamic subtitles and smooth transitions.
6. **Step 6: Thumbnail Design:**
   - Canva: High contrast background + human face + maximum 5 bold words.
