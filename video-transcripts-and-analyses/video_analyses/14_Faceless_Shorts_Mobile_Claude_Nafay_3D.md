## 3. The Money Math & Economic Reality
- **AdSense Reality Check on Shorts:**

  - Pinned monetization makes short-form content 30x more profitable than platform ad revenue alone.

## 4. Step-by-Step Implementation Blueprint
1. **Step 1: Mobile Script Generation with Claude:**
   - Prompt Claude mobile app:
     > *"Write 3 viral 30-second YouTube Shorts scripts in the 'Dark Psychology & Human Behavior' niche. Format: Hook (0-3s, high curiosity), 3 shocking psychological body points (3-20s), Call-to-action (20-30s). Keep sentences short and punchy."*
2. **Step 2: Voiceover Generation on Phone:**
   - Use ElevenLabs free mobile web tier or CapCut's built-in natural AI text-to-speech voices (e.g., "Narrator", "Energetic Male", "Calm Female").
3. **Step 3: Free Stock Background Clips:**
   - Download free 4K vertical clips from Pexels.com or Pixabay mobile browser (aesthetic moody cities, walking in rain, luxury cars, starry sky).
4. **Step 4: CapCut Mobile Editing (10 Minutes):**
   - Import video + voiceover.
   - Tap `Text` -> `Auto Captions` -> Select dynamic animated template (bouncing words, yellow highlights).
   - Add slow zoom-in on the video clip and subtle background sound effect (whoosh / bass drop).
5. **Step 5: Daily Distribution:**
   - Post to YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, and Facebook Reels simultaneously.
